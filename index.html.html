Introduction

Event Buddy ("the App", "we", "us") is an event management mobile application developed by N&D Lanka (Pvt) Ltd. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Event Buddy, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Do Not Collect

No GPS / location tracking No payment data No Firebase Analytics
  • We do not access or track your GPS or real-time location. Event location is a manually typed text field, not automatic geolocation.
  • We do not process payments, and no payment or financial information is collected.
  • We do not use Firebase Analytics or similar behavioral analytics/tracking services.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To let you browse, create, and manage events
  • To display your name, photo, and phone number on your profile as you choose to share them
  • To send push notifications about new or relevant events

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored using Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Firebase Cloud Firestore/Storage. Firebase applies industry-standard security practices to protect data in transit and at rest. While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

Third-Party Services

Event Buddy uses the following third-party service:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore/Storage, Cloud Messaging) — used to store account data, event data, images, and deliver push notifications. Firebase's own privacy practices apply to data processed on their infrastructure.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Event details you submit as an organizer (name, description, location, banner image) are visible to other users of the App as part of the App's core event-browsing functionality. Your profile name and photo may also be visible to other users where the App displays them.

Your Choices & Rights

  • You may edit or remove your phone number, bio, and profile photo at any time from your profile.
  • You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us using the details below.
  • You may disable notifications through your device's system settings.

Children's Privacy

Event Buddy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
